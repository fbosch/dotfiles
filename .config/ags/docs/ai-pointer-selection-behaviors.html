Each card applies a different geometric rule to the active gesture. The comparison makes accidental over-selection visible.

Production decision path

  1. Collect the complete bounded stroke until release.
  2. Derive one padded rectangle from the stroke extrema.
  3. Find accessible candidates throughout that rectangle, independent of the drawn path.
  4. Refine the capture only when the bounded candidate policy finds an unambiguous result.
