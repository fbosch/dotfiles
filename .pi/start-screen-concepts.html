All names, membership, usage, reset times, health results, and session metrics are fictional. No credentials or live account data were read. The compact 14-cell initial-context strip uses /context glyphs and category colors; its values and distribution are fictional, and ⛶ is free space. No health checks, language servers, or environment approvals were triggered.

Neovim appears only for launches through the configured integration. Its connection status includes workspace identity. LSP refers to Pi's disk-backed diagnostics, not Neovim's unsaved buffers; not checked, unavailable, and confirmed results stay distinct.

[next] marks the next candidate in the sample auth chain, not a promise of successful switching. Live selection must use the existing resolver and usage checks. Allowances belong to individual accounts and are not added together. Bars are rounded. Banked-reset values are samples; next expiry is distinct from allowance reset. "Not reported" does not mean zero or unlimited.

The empty editor and footer are identical in every concept. Scroll each terminal horizontally on narrow screens. This is an HTML-only preview; no Pi settings or extension behavior changed. Branch and paths are sample values; linked paths appear only for linked worktrees. Auth usage is shown in the agreed Pi-rendered Markdown table.
